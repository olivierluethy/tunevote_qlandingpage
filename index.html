Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TuneVote

What is TuneVote?

TuneVote is a collaborative music platform where friends can create or join sessions, suggest songs, and vote in real-time on what plays next. The song with the most votes wins and plays automatically – perfect democratic playlists!

Do I need a Spotify or YouTube account?

No, you don't need any of these accounts. The host needs a TuneVote account to create and public or private sessions. For joining an existing session, no account is needed. Participants only need to join via the link – no account required for voting.

Is TuneVote free to use?

Yes! Creating and joining public sessions is completely free. Artists and DJs can optionally create paid exclusive sessions where fans subscribe or tip – that’s how creators can earn money.

How does voting work?

Everyone in the session can vote 👍 Yes, 👎 No, or 🤷 IDK on suggested songs. The song with the highest net score (Yes minus No) wins. In case of a tie, our smart tie-breaker randomly picks a winner with a dramatic countdown.

Can I use TuneVote for parties, road trips, or workouts?

Absolutely! TuneVote is perfect for any group setting: house parties, long car rides, gym sessions, office background music, or even virtual hangouts. Everyone gets a voice in the playlist.

How can artists or DJs earn money with TuneVote?

Verified artists and DJs can host exclusive paid sessions. Fans subscribe monthly or tip during live performances. You keep most of the revenue – a new way to monetize your fanbase directly.

Still have questions?

Feel free to reach out to us at support@tunevote.com or join our community on Discord. We're happy to help!
